# Pumpios V8 Production Rules

## Status

- The 24 images in this folder are art-directed representative previews.
- They are not minted assets and are not final collection metadata.
- The remaining 1,176 IDs retain deterministic draft traits only.
- Production completion is `0 / 1,200` until the full set is rendered, reviewed, frozen, uploaded, and bound to a deployed collection.

## Canonical Shape

- Square portrait with a tall capsule head occupying roughly 48-58% of the frame height.
- Cream upper shell and a clearly separated colored lower shell remain the normal collection identity.
- Eyes stay between 30-52% of image height so the expression survives a circular avatar crop.
- Upper torso fills the lower third. Hands and one primary prop may enter from the lower corners.
- The capsule silhouette must remain identifiable at 64px without relying on an accessory.

## Drawing Language

- Confident black contour with small pressure and alignment variations.
- Mostly flat color, one cel-shadow family, and selective hand-hatched material detail.
- Texture belongs to specific surfaces. Do not wash the entire image in scratches.
- Ordinary expressions should vary through pupil scale, brow angle, mouth construction, and asymmetry.
- Backgrounds use direct-flash, scanner, photocopy, CRT, or low-resolution cues selectively.

## Rarity Direction

- Standard: one memorable face, one specific outfit collision, one clear prop, restrained setting.
- Rare: unusual material or narrative condition that changes the character concept.
- Super Rare: one impossible capsule material plus a strongly authored environment.
- Legendary: individually art-directed composition with unique prop and scene logic.
- Chairman: unique silhouette and personality without relying on a crown or label.

## Compatibility

- Transparent shells require an intentional suspended object and reduced clothing noise.
- Large eyewear cannot cover the primary expression; brows or mouth must carry identity.
- Complex props stay below the eye line and may not obscure more than one third of the face.
- Detailed environments use quieter outfits; loud fashion uses simpler backgrounds.
- No trait may introduce third-party logos, readable brand names, serials, or rarity text into artwork.

## Review Gates

1. Full-resolution anatomy and edge cleanup.
2. 256px composition check.
3. 128px expression check.
4. 64px silhouette check.
5. Circular crop check.
6. Contact-sheet duplicate and palette check.
7. Manifest-to-image trait review.
8. Metadata and gallery image equality check.
